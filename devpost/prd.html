Coin Garden

Product requirements: what you see, what you do, what you get back

📈 From scope to product

Scope said "buy a coin, plant a seed, watch it grow." Now we know exactly what that means: one garden screen, a notebook that is both log and guide, five stages, lessons above the flowers, a 10-second BTC Demo, and a "!" seed when prices can't be found.

🌿 Features and Behavior

🌱 The Garden

Each purchase is one plant; two buys of the same coin make two plants. The stage comes from gain over the buy price:

GainStage
Loss, or below +25%🌰 Seed (never wilts)
+25%🌱 Sprout, 2 leaves
+50%🌿 Taller, 4 leaves
+75%🌾 Bud
x2 or more🌻 Golden sunflower

How we'll know it works

  • An empty garden shows the welcome line, faint soil rows, the sign, Demo and 📖, with no numbers.
  • After saving a purchase, a seed appears without a reload.
  • A plant at each threshold shows the matching stage.
  • A plant below its buy price looks like a seed with no wilting.
📖 The Notebook

Opens from 📖, or from the sign directly on the new-entry page. Pages:

  1. How your garden grows: stages and the gain each one needs.
  2. What a seed can become: a sunflower in full bloom, captioned "Your seed can become this."
  3. New entry: coin, buy price, amount, date. Closing after a valid entry plants the seed.
  4. One page per plant: the recorded details, current gain once known, and a lesson field.

How we'll know it works

  • 📖 opens from anywhere; the sign opens the new-entry page.
  • An entry can't be saved without all four fields; price and amount must be above zero.
  • Tapping a plant opens its own page with exactly what was entered.
  • A written lesson is still there after reopening the app.
📜 Lessons

One lesson per plant, written on its notebook page. Tapping a sunflower shows it in a bubble above the flower. If the flower has none, and for the Demo plant, a built-in lesson appears:

  • "We don't have to be smarter than the rest. We have to be more disciplined than the rest." — Warren Buffett (attribution to verify)
  • "Plants need time to grow; fruit doesn't appear in a day."
  • A sunflower with a written lesson shows that lesson above it.
  • A sunflower without one shows a built-in lesson.
📈 Live Prices

When the app opens, the garden looks up each coin's current market price and plants update to match.

  • With a known coin and a working connection, the plant's stage matches the current gain.
▶ Demo

Pressing Demo plants a special BTC plant and simulates its price rising to x2 over about 10 seconds. It ends as a sunflower, tinted and labelled "Demo", showing a built-in lesson when tapped. Real plants are never touched.

  • The BTC plant goes seed → sprout → 4 leaves → bud → sunflower within about 10 seconds.
  • Afterward it is visibly marked "Demo" and real plants are unchanged.

🚦 States and Boundaries

StateWhat the investor sees
First useEmpty plot, welcome line, sign, Demo, 📖. No numbers.
Normal usePlants at their stages; the sign disappears after the first seed.
Price not found / offlinePlant stays a seed with a "!"; its page explains the price couldn't be found. It updates once a price is available.
Invalid entryThe notebook names the missing or invalid field; no seed is planted.
PersistencePurchases, lessons and the Demo plant remain on this device. Plants are never lost.

🧑‍🌾 Product Decisions

  • Your own empty plot first; the full-bloom image lives in the notebook as a promise.
  • Only two controls: 📖 and Demo. No accounts, so no "save progress" split.
  • The notebook is log and guide in one storybook object.
  • Losses stay seeds. No wilting.
  • Lessons per plant appear above the flower when tapped, with built-in fallback.
  • Demo is its own 10-second BTC plant that stays as a tinted "Demo" sunflower.
  • Missing price means a seed with "!", an honest signal instead of a broken screen.
  • Visual style only from the reference app.
  • Assumption: pressing Demo again replays the single demo plant instead of adding more.
  • Assumption: the name stays "Coin Garden" unless renamed.
Deferred
  • Selling / marking plants sold: not needed to prove the growing garden.
  • Editing or deleting entries: not discussed; add after the core loop works.
  • Multiple devices: would need accounts or sync.
Non-Goals
  • Up/Down calls, leaderboards, tiers, equipment
  • Exchange or wallet syncing
  • Wilting or dying plants
  • Charts, portfolio totals, trading advice
Open Questions
  • Buffett quote source: verify before shipping. Doesn't block spec.
  • How a coin is entered (free text, ticker, or picked from a list): shapes when "price not found" happens. The learner should confirm the experience.
