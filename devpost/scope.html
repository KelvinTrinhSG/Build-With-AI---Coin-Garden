Coin Garden

Every crypto purchase is a seed. Patience grows it into a sunflower.

Scope · approved · working title

🌻 The Unique Kernel

Buy-and-hold investing shown as a gentle, patient garden instead of a stressful chart. Each purchase is a seed. It grows only as the price rises toward x2 and blooms into a golden sunflower at x2. A loss never kills it; it just stays a seed. Flowers hold lessons about patience and discipline.

🔁 The Core Loop

Investor → writes a purchase in the notebook → a seed appears → price (live or Demo) sets the gain → the plant grows toward a sunflower → tap any plant to reopen its notebook page.

🧑‍🌾 Who It's For

The learner: a long-term buy-and-hold crypto investor who also loves games like Stardew Valley. Today, their purchase history lives on exchanges and in numbers. They want a personal, visual record that rewards patience instead of feeding the urge to check charts.

💛 Why It Matters

The garden turns a habit they care about, patient buy-and-hold, into something calm and rewarding to look at. It is also practice toward their learning goals: building with AI, clean architecture, and product design that is pleasant for users.

🎬 What "Working" Looks Like

The learner opens the app and writes a purchase in the notebook. A seed appears in the garden. They press Demo, which simulates the price rising, and they watch their plant grow stage by stage: sprout, 4 leaves, bud, then a golden sunflower at x2. Tapping the plant shows its notebook entry.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the seed growing into a sunflower in front of you.

🎨 Inspiration & Identity

Stardew Valley for the cozy farming mood. The visual style is borrowed from another farm app the learner described, visuals only, none of its mechanics:

  • A warm, rounded picture-book look in earthy colours, all defined in one single theme file.
  • Fredoka for headings, Nunito for body text.
  • "Chunky" wooden-toy UI: thick 3px brown borders, large radius, solid 4–6px drop shadow. Buttons press down 4px when clicked.
  • Titles painted onto the scene in cream text with a brown shadow, like a wooden sign.
  • One isometric SVG farm world sits behind the UI, with panels floating on top of it.

These swatches are approximations for this page; exact values get set in the PRD/spec.

✂️ The Boundary

Now In the proof of concept
  • Notebook entry: coin, buy price, amount, date.
  • A garden scene in the picture-book style, with one plant per purchase.
  • Five growth stages driven by gain over the buy price.
  • Current price from a free price API (CoinGecko or similar).
  • Demo button that simulates a price rise so growth can be seen in seconds.
  • Tap a plant to view its notebook entry.
  • Two built-in lessons.
  • The garden is saved between visits, so plants are never lost.
Later Worth doing, just not now
  • Selling a coin, and writing your own lesson into the flower at the moment you sell.
  • Sold plants stay in the garden forever, marked as sold.
  • Richer farm progression: fences, raised beds, a more detailed isometric world.
Cut Explicitly left out
  • Up/Down calls, leaderboards, tiers, buyable equipment — the learner only wants the reference app's visual style, not its mechanics.
  • Syncing with exchanges or wallets — manual notebook entry proves the idea and avoids API keys and credentials.
  • Wilting or dying plants on losses — goes against the kernel; losses simply stay seeds.
